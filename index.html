About Us

Weatherly Direct Primary Care is a membership-based primary care practice led by Sarah McGinley in Wakefield, Rhode Island. We proudly serve South Kingstown and nearby communities, providing personalized healthcare for patients aged 16 and older. With more than 14 years of experience in South County healthcare, we are committed to helping our patients feel supported and cared for.

Our services include preventive care, sick visits, annual wellness exams, women's health and menopause care, chronic disease management, behavioral health support, telehealth, physicals, and in-office testing. We also coordinate with specialists when additional care is needed.

We believe healthcare should be personal, convenient, and unhurried. Our membership-based model offers longer appointments and direct access to your clinician without insurance billing between you and your care. To learn more about online resources, visit GitHub.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who can become a patient at Weatherly Direct Primary Care?

We welcome patients aged 16 and older in Wakefield, South Kingstown, and surrounding Rhode Island communities.

2. What services does the practice offer?

We offer preventive care, sick visits, women's health, menopause care, chronic disease management, telehealth, annual wellness exams, and other primary care services.

3. What are the benefits of membership-based primary care?

Membership provides longer appointments, direct access to your clinician, and personalized care without insurance billing between you and your provider.
